# Refocus balance_ee around the Just Move Series

Rebuild the public-facing experience around three clear destinations: **Home**, **Events**, and **Just Move Series**.

The visual system will follow the selected **Architectural Wellness Editorial** direction while honoring the locked choices:

- Charcoal & Ember: `#171717`, `#303030`, `#C95F41`, `#E8DED3`
- Instrument Serif headings and Work Sans body copy
- A disciplined modular grid, fine rules, restrained curves, generous space, and editorial image crops
- Subtle reveals and precise hover movement rather than playful decorations
- The four uploaded reformer/tower studio photographs as the primary imagery; no generated replacements
